# Email to MN DNR — Lake Bathymetry Data Use Request

**To:** MN DNR Fisheries / Ecological and Water Resources — Lake Mapping Program
**CC:** gisinfo.mngeo@state.mn.us
**Subject:** Commercial Data Use Agreement Request — Lake Bathymetry Data for OpenCatch Fishing App

Dear Minnesota DNR Lake Mapping Program,

I am writing to request permission to use MN DNR lake bathymetry data in OpenCatch, a consumer fishing application currently under development. I would also like to clarify the terms of the DNR's General Data and Software License Agreement as they apply to our intended use.

## About OpenCatch

OpenCatch is a mobile application designed to help anglers discover and evaluate fishing locations across Minnesota and beyond. The app provides weather-based fishing forecasts, lake information, and mapping features to improve the angling experience. A core part of our mission is promoting informed, conservation-minded fishing by helping users understand lake environments — including depth profiles, underwater structure, and habitat characteristics.

## Intended Use of DNR Lake Bathymetry Data

We would like to incorporate the following DNR datasets into OpenCatch:

1. **Lake outline polygons** — to identify and display lake boundaries within the app.
2. **Depth contour lines** — to render bathymetric maps that help anglers understand underwater topography.
3. **Digital Elevation Models (DEMs)** — to generate depth-shaded visualizations and support analytical features.

Additionally, we use bathymetric data as training input for machine learning models that predict lake depth profiles for unmapped water bodies. These models produce derivative depth estimates (not reproductions of DNR survey data) and would be clearly distinguished from official DNR measurements.

## Specific Questions

To ensure we comply with DNR data use requirements, I would appreciate clarification on the following:

1. **Commercial use agreement:** We understand that commercial reproduction or utilization of DNR lake data requires a written agreement. We have reviewed the agreement form at files.dnr.state.mn.us/eco/lakemapping/agreement.pdf and are prepared to execute it. Are there additional steps or fees involved?

2. **Derivative products:** Does the data use agreement extend to derivative products, such as machine learning models trained on DNR sonar survey data? Our models produce independent depth predictions rather than displaying the original survey data directly.

3. **Attribution requirements:** What is the required attribution format? We plan to display a credit line (e.g., "Bathymetry data: Minnesota DNR") on every lake map that uses DNR data and are happy to follow any specific attribution guidelines you provide.

4. **Scope of permitted use:** Are there restrictions on charging end users for access to an app that displays or derives value from DNR bathymetric data?

5. **Data redistribution:** Does the agreement permit redistribution of processed or reformatted versions of DNR data (e.g., vector tiles generated from contour shapefiles), provided proper attribution is maintained?

## Our Commitment

We have great respect for the decades of survey work the DNR Lake Mapping Program has conducted. Minnesota's lake bathymetry dataset is among the most comprehensive in the nation, and we want to ensure our use of it is fully authorized and properly attributed. We are committed to:

- Displaying clear attribution to the Minnesota DNR on all maps derived from your data.
- Never misrepresenting derivative predictions as official DNR measurements.
- Complying fully with the terms of any data use agreement.
- Supporting lake conservation messaging within the app.
